# TipShare by Ponchi — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 15, 2026
**Last updated:** September 15, 2026

> **DRAFT — for attorney review before publication.**

This Privacy Policy describes how Optima BuildSolutions LLC ("**TipShare**," "**we**," "**us**"),
the developer of the TipShare by Ponchi application (the "**App**"), collects, uses, and
protects information when a merchant installs the App from the Clover App Market or uses
our related websites and services.

TipShare is a tool for **merchants** (business owners and their authorized managers). The
merchant — not TipShare — decides what employee information is processed and how tips are
distributed. For most data we process, we act as a service provider/processor on the
merchant's behalf.

## 1. Information we collect

**From Clover, with the merchant's authorization.** When a merchant installs the App and
grants permissions through Clover's authorization flow, we receive, via the Clover API:

- **Payment and order data**: payment amounts, taxes, tips, service charges, refunds,
  order line items (names and prices), timestamps, and the identifier of the employee who
  processed each payment. We do **not** receive or store full card numbers, card
  verification data, or cardholder authentication data.
- **Employee data**: employee names, Clover employee identifiers, roles, and — where the
  merchant uses Clover's time clock — shift clock-in/clock-out times.
- **Merchant data**: business name, merchant identifier, and App Market subscription status.

**From Ponchi, if the merchant links a Ponchi account**: employee rosters and time-clock
records (clock-in/out times, hours) from the merchant's Ponchi workspace.

**Uploaded by the merchant**: time-clock or payroll files (for example, CSV punch reports
from third-party time clocks), which may contain employee names and hours.

**Entered in the App**: tip-distribution rules, role assignments, manual adjustments,
bonuses, and notes.

**Automatically**: standard technical logs (IP address, browser/device type, pages viewed,
timestamps) used for security and troubleshooting.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. The App is a business tool
and is not directed at children.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information above solely to provide the service:

- calculating tip distributions, tip-outs, and pooled amounts under the merchant's rules;
- attributing sales, tips, gratuities, and hours to employees and shifts;
- producing reports and payroll-ready exports for the merchant;
- showing employees their own tip amounts, **only if the merchant enables that feature**;
- operating, securing, maintaining, and improving the App;
- providing customer support and communicating about the service.

**We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for
cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use merchant or employee data to train
artificial-intelligence models.**

## 3. AI-assisted setup

The App offers an optional AI-assisted setup that helps merchants describe their
tip-distribution rules in plain language. When a merchant uses this feature, the text of
the merchant's answers (and, if the merchant uploads one, the contents of their existing
tip-calculation spreadsheet) is processed by our AI subprocessor, Anthropic, PBC, to
generate a proposed rules configuration. Payroll calculations themselves are always
performed by our own deterministic software — never by an AI model. Per our agreement with
Anthropic, data submitted through the API is not used to train Anthropic's models.

## 4. How we share information

We share information only with the subprocessors needed to run the service:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Railway Corp. (railway.app) | Application hosting and database storage |
| Clover / Fiserv | The App operates on the Clover platform; billing is handled by Clover |
| Anthropic, PBC | AI-assisted setup (Section 3) — only when the merchant uses that feature |
| Stripe (Ponchi) | Only if the merchant subscribes to Ponchi services billed by us directly |

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users'
safety, or in connection with a merger or sale of the business (in which case this policy
continues to apply to previously collected data).

We never share one merchant's data with another merchant.

## 5. Employee information

Employee names, hours, sales, and tip amounts are processed **on behalf of the merchant**,
who is the employer and decides how tips are distributed. Employees with questions about
how their information is used, or who wish to access or correct it, should contact their
employer. We support merchants in fulfilling such requests.

## 6. Data retention

We retain merchant and employee data while the merchant's subscription is active, because
tip and payroll history is the core function of the App. After a merchant uninstalls the
App or closes their account, we delete or de-identify their data within **90 days**,
except where a longer period is required by law or needed to resolve billing disputes.
Merchants may request earlier deletion at contact@ponchi.app.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, scoped
API credentials, access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel, and audit
logging of tip-rule changes. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected
merchants of any breach involving their data as required by applicable law.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your state (including under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the
Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or
receive a copy of personal information, and to not be discriminated against for exercising
those rights. Merchants may exercise these rights at contact@ponchi.app. Employees should
direct requests to their employer (see Section 5); we will assist the employer in
responding. We do not sell personal information, so no opt-out of sale is needed.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new
effective date and, for material changes, notify merchants through the App or by email.
